The grocery lines tell the longest story. At the start they are full of things for small children. [example needed, an early grocery item]. Then the amounts go up. More of everything, in bigger packs. Then, one sheet at a time, the lines get shorter again. The children grew up. Then they left. The list noticed first.

So the box is not a record of jobs. It is a record of us. I did not write it as one. That is why I trust it.

This morning I put the lid back on the box. The newest sheet is still on the fridge. It is short. It has room for more.
