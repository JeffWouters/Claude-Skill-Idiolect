The tap has dripped for [number needed] weeks. I fixed it this morning. The fix took minutes. Getting ready took the rest of the time.

The fault was the washer. A small rubber seal at the base of the tap. It wears flat and hard. Then it stops sealing and the tap drips. Not a flood. Just one drop, then the next, all night.

So first the shop. The hardware shop in [place needed]. They sell washers in a small bag, a mix of sizes. [price needed] for the bag. I bought two. Cheap is not the point. Having the right size is.

Then the water. The main valve under the sink, off. Then the tap on, to let the last of it drain. I put the plug in the sink. Small parts go down plug holes. I have learnt that one the hard way.

Here is the order of the job. Cap off the top. The screw under the cap out. Handle off. Then the spanner on the big nut and the inner part out. The old washer sits at the base. Flat and worn. I pressed the new one on in its place.

Then back the way it came. Slowly. I laid each part on a tea towel in the order it came off. That is the trick. If you do that, you cannot put it back wrong. I did the nut up snug. Not hard. Hard strips the thread.

Then the water back on. A quarter turn on the valve. Wait. Listen. Then a bit more. Water comes back with a bang if you rush it. I ran the tap. I shut it. I watched the spout.

Nothing.

The job took about [number needed] minutes. Finding the spanner, going to the shop and clearing out the cupboard took far longer. It always does.

The spare washers are in a jar in the drawer by the sink. The spout is dry now. The tea towel is back on its rail.
