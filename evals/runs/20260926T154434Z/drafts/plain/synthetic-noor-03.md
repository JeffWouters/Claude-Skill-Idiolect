First, the drain. The parts inside a faucet like this are small, and the kitchen sink drain is exactly the right size to swallow one of them. I plugged it and laid a cloth in the bottom of the basin, so that anything I dropped would land softly and stay put.

Next, the water supply. I shut off the valves under the sink so no water could reach the faucet while it was open. Then I opened the tap to empty the line. Whatever water was still sitting in the pipe ran out into the sink rather than into my face once the faucet came apart.
