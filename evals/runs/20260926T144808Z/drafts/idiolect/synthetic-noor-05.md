Then the weave. Now the needle goes crosswise. Over one thread, under the next. All the way across. Then back the other way, under where it went over. Row by row, the gap closes. It is slow. It is meant to be slow. So I do not rush it.

The one thing to watch is the tension. Pull the yarn tight and the patch goes hard. A hard ridge under the heel. You feel it with every step. Not in the first hour. Later. That kind of discomfort is hard to ignore. So I keep it loose. Looser than you think. The patch should give a little, like the rest of the sock.
