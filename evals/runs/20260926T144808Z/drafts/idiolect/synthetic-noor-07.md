I could work on the train. Many people do. I do not.

That is a choice. The laptop stays shut. So I do one of two things. I rest, or I look out of the window.

Here is what the window gives me.

First the light. It is never the same for long. It shifts with the seasons. In the dark months the glass shows me the lit carriage and not much else. Then the mornings open up. Resting is harder than it sounds. It is not sleep. It is sitting still and letting the ride carry me. No emails. No lists. I am not always good at it. But it is the only part of the day that asks nothing of me.

And the routine does something else. It makes me notice. When you see the same thing every day, a small change stands out. You do not look for it. It finds you. [example needed, a small change seen from the train].
