The same chores come round again and again. Clean the oven. Descale the kettle. Sort the shoe rack. They appear, get crossed off, and appear again a few months later. It is like reading the seasons. Clear the gutters in autumn. Service the mower in spring.

Some tasks do not get crossed off. They sit there. They get copied from one sheet to the next. Fix the drawer that sticks. Reseal around the bath. The grocery part is the best part. It is a history of the family. Early on the list is short. Coffee, bread, milk, wine. Then nappies appear. Then baby rice. Then those small yoghurts in packs of six, on every sheet, for years. Then the yoghurts go. Cereal changes. The loaves get bigger. Milk goes from one bottle to two. Later there is a stretch of a particular crisp that someone went through a phase with. Then it stops. Nobody announced it. It just stopped being on the list.

You cannot see a family change when you are in it. You can see it on the fridge sheets. It is all there in biro.

And then there is the hall. Paint the hallway. It is on a sheet from years ago, near the top of the box. Crossed out, then written in again. It turns up every so often all the way through. Sometimes it is underlined. Once it has a question mark.
