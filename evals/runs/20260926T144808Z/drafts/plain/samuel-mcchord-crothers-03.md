He was young, and he was not who he said he was. He had taken a false name, and under it he decided to become a preacher. It was not a calling he had trained for or, as far as anyone could tell, one he had thought much about before trying it.

Before his first sermon he was badly nervous. He had no idea whether he could do it, and the congregation had no reason to be patient with him if he failed. Then he stood up, began to speak, and found that the words came easily. They kept coming. By the time he sat down he had surprised himself, and he came away half convinced that he had been called. If the speaking came so naturally, he reasoned, perhaps he was meant for it.

Others seemed to agree. A parish nearby heard of him and hired him as its minister. He settled in and did the work, and in one part of it he became something of a success.
