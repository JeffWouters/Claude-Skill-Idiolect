Now the crosswise threads. The lengthwise ones are already in, a row of parallel lines over the hole. Now you go the other way. Over one, under one. Then back again, under where you went over. That is the weave. It is slow. It is meant to be.

The one thing to watch is the tension. Do not pull the threads tight. It is tempting. A tight thread looks neat. But a tight patch puckers. The sock goes on, the heel stretches, and the patch drags on the good wool round it. Then that gives instead. Leave each thread a little slack. It should lie flat and give when you press it.

When the gap is filled, take the ends back into the wool on the inside. Trim them. Turn the sock the right way out. Done.

I did not expect to like this. I do. It is restful. You cannot hurry it, and there is nothing to decide. Over, under, back. [time needed] for a heel. It is one of the few times I sit still.
