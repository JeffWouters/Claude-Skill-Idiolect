Subject: How a gentleman's code of honor complements religious moral teaching.
- Religion prescribes repentance after wrongdoing, while honor guides repairing harm done to others.
- Because Christian ideals demand perfection nobody attains, believers are unpredictable; honor's limited, explicit rules make a gentleman reliable and possible to follow lifelong.
- Honor's worldliness is a strength, not a fault.
Length: about 390 words
