Then I read the log. Most of them have dried a little, mostly at the ends. Fine cracks run out from the centre like spokes. They show where the wood wants to split. So I stand the log on the block and turn it until the biggest crack lines up with my swing. I aim for that crack. Not the middle.

The swing itself is simple. Feet apart. Hands together at the end of the handle. I lift the maul straight up over my head. Then I let it drop, and pull it down through the log. I do not try to force it. The weight of the head does most of the work. A clean hit and the log falls open in two.

The knotty ones are different. The maul just bounces off them. So I tap the wedge into the best crack I can find. Then I drive it in with [example needed: what I strike the wedge with]. Slowly. The log creaks and opens a little at a time.
