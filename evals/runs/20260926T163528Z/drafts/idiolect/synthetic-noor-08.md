Pick an overcast day. Not a sunny one. In sun the solution dries on the glass before you can wipe it. It leaves streaks, and you chase them round the pane. On a grey day it stays wet long enough to work.

So I start at the top of the house. Top floor first, then down, room by room. That way any drips land on glass I have not done yet. Not on glass I have just finished.

Frames before glass, every window. The frames hold dust, and plenty of it. Wipe the glass first and the frame drops it all back on. So the cloth goes round the frame. Then the glass.

Screw a sheet of paper into a ball. Dip it in the solution and squeeze it out. Wipe the pane in circles, then finish top to bottom. Then a dry sheet, to polish. Keep going until the glass squeaks.

You know the paper is spent when it goes soft. It stops shining the glass and starts to smear it. Then it goes in the bin and I screw up a fresh sheet.
