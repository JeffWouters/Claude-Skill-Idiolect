I clean the windows with vinegar and old newspaper. It is cheap and it works. You need little. A bucket of water with vinegar in it. [number needed: how much vinegar to how much water]. A cloth or sponge for the wet part. A stack of old newspaper for the dry part. [example needed: anything else, such as a ladder or step]. That is the whole kit.

Pick an overcast day. This sounds wrong. It is not. In full sun, the glass dries before you can wipe it. You get streaks. On a grey day, you have time. The light is flat as well, so you can see what you have missed.

Start at the top. Do the top floor first and work down. Drips run down. If you do the ground floor first, the upstairs drips land on clean glass. Then you do it twice.

On each window, do the frames before the glass. The frames hold the dirt. Dead flies, dust, cobweb, grit in the corners. Wipe them down and let them dry. If you do the glass first, you drag frame dirt onto it when you do the frames. Frames, then glass.

Wet the glass with the cloth. Not dripping. Then take a sheet of newspaper and screw it up into a loose ball. Wipe in circles first, to lift the dirt. Then finish with straight strokes, top to bottom. Turn the ball as you go so you are always on a dry part.

You can tell when the paper is spent. It goes soft and grey. It stops squeaking on the glass and starts to smear. Throw it away and take a fresh sheet.
