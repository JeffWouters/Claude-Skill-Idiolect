The swing is simple. But it is easy to rush. Feet apart, square to the block. I lift the maul up over my head. Then I let it drop and pull it through. I do not try to smash it. The weight of the head does the work. My job is to hit the right spot.

Most logs open on the first or second go. Some do not. The knotty ones are stubborn. The grain winds round the knot and holds everything tightly together. For those I use the wedge. I tap it into a crack until it stands up by itself. Then I drive it in hard. It creaks. Then, gradually, it gives.
