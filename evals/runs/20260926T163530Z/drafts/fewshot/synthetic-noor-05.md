Turning needs the right weather. Not wet. Wet compost is heavy and it sticks to everything, the fork, the boots, the gloves. Not frozen either. A frozen top is hard work and gets you nowhere. So I wait for a dry spell. A dry, mild day is best. Cool enough to work. Dry enough that the heap breaks apart when you lift it.
