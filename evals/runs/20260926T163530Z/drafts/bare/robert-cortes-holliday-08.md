When I mentioned this to my friends they thought it the funniest thing they had heard in a month.  [example needed: what the friends said]  I laughed too, of course.  But on the way home it occurred to me to wonder why it should be so very comic.  We are perfectly willing to make jokes about death, which is a serious matter; yet the undertaker, who is merely the man who attends to the arrangements, we regard with a sort of shudder.  We would rather not have him to dinner.  We would decidedly rather not have him next door.

This last point, my host tells me, is a common belief and very often a mistaken one.  People are convinced, almost everywhere, that an establishment of his kind must lower the value of every house, shop and lot in the neighbourhood.
