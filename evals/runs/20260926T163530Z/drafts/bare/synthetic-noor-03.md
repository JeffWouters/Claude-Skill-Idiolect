The saucers go in the freezer before anything else. I need them cold when the time comes. Then the pan goes on. The fruit and sugar have to reach a rolling boil. Not a simmer. A proper boil that you cannot stir down. Then I wait for the set. This part takes nerve. Too soon and it stays runny. Too late and it turns dark and stiff.

To test it I put a spoonful on a cold saucer. I give it a minute. Then I push it with my finger. If the skin wrinkles, it is set. If it runs, the pan goes back on and I try again a few minutes later.

The jars go into a low oven while the pan boils. They have to be warm when the marmalade goes in, or they can crack. I fill them almost to the top with a ladle and a jam funnel. Then the lids go straight on, while everything is still hot. Later they pop down one by one as they cool.
