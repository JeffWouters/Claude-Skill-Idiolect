I regrouted a tiled wall at home. [example needed: which wall]. The old grout was past saving. I had been going to do it for a long time. Longer than I like to admit.

First the joints. I raked out the old grout. Not all of it. Down far enough for the new grout to grip. Then I cleared the dust out of every line. Then a damp cloth over the lot. The tiles have to be clean and just damp. Not wet.

Then the mixing. This is where people go wrong. You add powder to water, not the other way round. You mix it stiff. Then you leave it to stand for a few minutes. Then you mix it again. It is slower. It is also the only way to get grout that holds.

I pressed it in with a rubber float, held at an angle. Across the joints, not along them. Small patches at a time. Then the sponge. Wrung out hard. Light passes, in circles, then one clean wipe.
