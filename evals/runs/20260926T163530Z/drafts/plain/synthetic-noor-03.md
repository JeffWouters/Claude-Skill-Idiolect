Once the sugar has dissolved into the softened peel and juice, I turn the heat right up and let the pan come to a hard, rolling boil. This is the stage that needs watching. Before I start I put [number needed] saucers in the freezer, and after [time needed] of boiling I take the pan off the heat, drop a spoonful onto a chilled saucer and wait a minute. If the surface wrinkles when I push it with a fingertip, it has reached setting point. If it stays runny, the pan goes back on and I test again a few minutes later. Getting the set wrong either way is the main risk: too soon and it pours like syrup, too late and it turns dark and stiff.

When it sets, I let it stand for a few minutes so the peel settles evenly, then ladle it into jars that have been warmed in the oven, so the hot marmalade does not crack them. Each jar is filled close to the top and sealed straight away while everything is still warm.
