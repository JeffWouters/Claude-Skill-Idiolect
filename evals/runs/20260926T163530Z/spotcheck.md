A father reads to his children things they will not appreciate for many years. A mother insists on standards of speech and manners that win her no thanks. None of this earns money, and very little of it earns flattery. Cultivation is, in the plainest commercial sense, a bad investment. That is exactly why it is rare, and why it is so easily lost when a society begins to judge everything by what it pays.

There is one more symptom worth naming.
