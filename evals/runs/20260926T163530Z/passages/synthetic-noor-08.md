Three years ago I went out on the ladder for that corner. The ladder stood in the soft bed under the window. One foot sank. The whole thing leaned left. I came down slowly and put the ladder away.

That strip waits for the window cleaner. He comes once a year with a long pole and a hose. He charges 35 euros for the whole house, outside only.

Downstairs is easy. I do the outside from the path and the inside from a kitchen chair. The big window in the front room is two metres wide. It takes six sheets of newspaper on its own. I do it last, when the bucket is cold.

Hanna watched from over the fence while I did her side of the kitchen window. The side that faces her garden. Later she said, you missed a bit. She was right. A grey arc at the top of the landing window, where my arm did not go.

Here is the count from last Saturday.

11 windows. 38 half sheets of newspaper. One bucket of water, changed once. Two hours and 40 minutes, with a stop for tea at 11.15. One dead wasp on the landing sill. One grey arc.

But I could pay for all of it. The window cleaner would do the inside too, for another 20 euros. I know that. I have known it for years.

I keep doing it because of the week after. The rooms look bigger. The garden comes right up to the glass. The blackbird on the fence looks close enough to touch.

And the paper still does it better than anything I have bought.

This evening the sun came low across the back garden at about 18.40. It lit every pane in the kitchen and showed nothing at all. Upstairs, the one strip in the back bedroom. Pale and dusty along the bottom, waiting for the pole.
