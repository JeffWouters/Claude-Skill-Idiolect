Here is how a sheet works. When a job is done, it gets a line through it. Not rubbed out. Struck out, so you can still read it. When a job is not done, it moves. It gets copied onto the next sheet. Then onto the one after that, if need be.

That rule is what makes the pile worth reading. The struck-out lines show what got done. The copied lines show what did not.
