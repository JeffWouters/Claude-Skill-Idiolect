There is a sheet of paper on our fridge. There has been one for years. Not the same sheet. They fill up and get replaced. But always a sheet, always in the same place, held up by the same magnet.

It has three parts. Groceries at the top. Quick jobs in the middle. Big projects at the bottom.

Groceries is simple. If you use the last of something, you write it down. That is the one rule. Whoever finishes the milk writes milk. Whoever takes the last tin writes tins. Not the person who notices later. The person who used it up. It works because it happens at the moment you know. Not an hour later, when you have forgotten.

Quick jobs are the things that take ten minutes and never get done. [example needed] [example needed] They sit on the list until someone has ten minutes and nothing to do. Then they go.

Big projects are the slow ones. [example needed] They can sit at the bottom of the sheet for a long time. That is fine. At least they are written down. At least nobody can say they forgot.

I have tried other ways. I tried apps on the phone. They were fine for me. Nobody else opened them. I tried a notebook. It lived in a drawer, and a list in a drawer is not a list. I tried a whiteboard. It got wiped. Things vanished from it that nobody had done. Nobody knew who had wiped it or why.

Paper on the fridge beats all of them. For one reason. You can see it. You go to the fridge ten times a day. Every time, the list is there, looking at you. You do not have to remember to check it. You cannot avoid it. And it costs nothing. A sheet of paper and a pen on a string.

It is not clever. It is not new. It just works. Which is more than I can say for the others.
