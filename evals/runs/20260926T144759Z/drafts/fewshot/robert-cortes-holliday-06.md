Consider the person who wants a present.  He comes in, and he wants a book for somebody, and he does not know what.  You suggest something.  No, he does not think that would do.  You suggest something else.  No, not that.  You suggest a third thing, and a fourth, and a fifth.  He is very polite about it.  He looks at each one and he puts it down.  He has, it appears, a very clear idea of what he does not want, and no idea whatever of what he does.  After half an hour or so he goes away, thanking you, to try somewhere else.  I have never learned what he gave.

That is one customer.  There are a great many others, and they come all in the same day, one on the heels of another, and the bookshop assistant must meet each of them where he stands.  One moment a learned gentleman is asking after an edition of [example needed], with notes; the next, a young woman wishes to know whether we have anything about the moving pictures.  You go from the one to the other without a breath between.  You must be scholarly, and then you must be frivolous, and then you must be scholarly again; and all the time you must keep your countenance.

I will set down, as nearly as I can remember them, some of the things that I have been asked for.  A book on the care of the baby.  A book on the care of the teeth.  Something to read on the train; not too long, as the gentleman was going only as far as [place].  Something to read on the steamer; rather long, as the lady was going abroad.  A book in a red binding, to go with a room.  A book that a lady had seen in somebody's house, with a picture on the cover, of which she did not know the name nor the author.  A book on etiquette.  A book of jokes for a banquet.  A book for a boy who did not like books.  A book to give an invalid, "nothing sad."  A book on how to train a dog.  A book on how to be happy.  A book for a man who had everything.

Early and late, week in and week out, you give your mind to these things.  It is, you might say, a liberal education.  Whose education, I have never been quite sure.
