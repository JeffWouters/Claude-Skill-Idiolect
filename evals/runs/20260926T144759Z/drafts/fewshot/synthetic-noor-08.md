I keep the old sheets. The chore lists from the fridge. When one is full, it comes down and goes into a folder, and a new one goes up. There is a box of them now. Years of them.

The rule is simple. When a job is done, it gets struck out. One line through it. When a sheet is full and a job is still not done, it moves. It gets written again on the next sheet. And again on the one after, if it has to be.

That is what makes the box worth keeping. You can read it like a record.
