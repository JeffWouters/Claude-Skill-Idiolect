There is a list on my fridge. There has been a list on my fridge for 15 years. Not the same list. But always one.

It is held up by a magnet shaped like a lemon. The paper is A5, cut from an old pad. I write in pencil. Pencil can be rubbed out. And pencil does not run when the fridge door drips.

The list has three parts. I drew the lines with a ruler.

The top part is food. What we are out of. Milk, eggs, bread, the usual. Anyone in the house can add to it. The rule is: if you finish it, you write it.

The middle part is jobs. Small ones. Things that take less than an hour. Fix the loose hinge on the bathroom cupboard. Change the bulb on the stairs. Oil the back gate. Clean the filter in the washing machine.

The bottom part is big jobs. Things that need a day, or money, or help. Paint the hall. Clear the loft. Get the chimney swept.

So that is the system. It is not clever. But it works, and I have tried a lot of systems.

I have tried apps. Three of them. Each one worked for a month. Then I stopped opening it. An app is out of sight. A list on the fridge is not. You see it every time you want a drink of water. Eight or ten times a day. It nags you without saying a word.

I have tried a notebook. Better. But the notebook lived in a drawer. Same problem.

I have tried a whiteboard. It was good for a year. Then the pens dried out. And someone wiped it by leaning on it. I lost a whole week of jobs to the back of a coat.

So now it is paper and pencil.

Here is the part I like most. When a job is done, I do not rub it out. I cross it through. One firm line. So by the end of a week the list is full of lines. Some weeks, a lot of lines. Some weeks, two.

When the page is full, I take it down. I put it in a box file in the hall cupboard. Then I start a new one. I carry over the jobs that are not done.

The box file has about 200 old lists in it now. I looked through them last winter. It was strange. Like a diary, but only the dull parts.

And the dull parts told a story.

The same jobs came back again and again. Clean the gutter: twice a year, every year. Bleed the radiators: every October. The back gate: oiled 11 times in 15 years. It still squeaks.

Some jobs sat on the list for a long time. Paint the hall was carried over 34 times. That is more than two years. I did it in the end, in one weekend. It took less time than it took to keep writing it down.

Some jobs just vanished. Fix the doorbell. It sat there for months. Then one list it was gone. I have no memory of fixing it. I checked. The doorbell works. So I must have done it. Or someone did.

And the food part was the saddest and the best. You can see the years in it. Baby food, then fish fingers, then crisps and cola in large amounts. Then, for a few years, oat milk. Then less of everything, as the house got quieter.

I keep the lists for no good reason. I will not read them often. But I do not throw them out.

This morning the new list went up. Top part: milk, onions, bin bags. Middle part: fix the drip in the shower, sort the screw jars. Bottom part: paint the hall. Again.

The pencil hangs on a string from the lemon magnet. It is down to four centimetres. I sharpened it with the kitchen knife and hung it back.
