While it cooks I peel the carrots. I grate them on the coarse side of the box grater. Grated carrot vanishes into the soup and turns it sweet. Chopped carrot stays as carrot. Both are fine. I like grated.

And the ginger. A piece the size of my thumb to the first knuckle. I scrape the skin off with a teaspoon. Then I grate it fine, straight into the pot. The smell comes up at once. Sharp and warm.

Then the spices. One teaspoon of cumin. Half a teaspoon of turmeric. A little chilli if I have it. I stir them into the oil for 30 seconds. Spices need fat and heat to wake up. Dry spices in water taste of dust.

So then the lentils. 250 grams. I rinse them first in a sieve under the tap until the water runs clear. It takes about a minute. The first water is cloudy and full of fine grit.

The lentils go in with the carrots. Then 1.2 litres of water. Stock if I have it. Water if I do not. I have made it both ways and the difference is small.

Bring it up to a boil. Then down to a low bubble. Lid half on.

And now the long wait. 25 minutes. I set the kitchen timer. It is a white plastic egg that ticks. I like the tick. It tells me the soup is going on without me.

I do the washing up. I clear the counter. I lay the table. Two bowls if someone is coming. One if not.
